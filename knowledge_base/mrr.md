# MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

**Definition:** 
MRR is the total predictable revenue generated by active subscriptions each month.

**Formula:**
`SUM(monthly_fee) WHERE subscriptions.status = 'active' AND subscriptions.status != 'trial'`

**Notes:**
- Do not include one-off invoices or taxes in MRR.
- Only look at the `subscriptions` table.
